# Code of Conduct

ProofGarden follows the Contributor Covenant spirit: be respectful, constructive, and careful with one another.

## Expected Behavior

- Use welcoming and inclusive language.
- Respect different backgrounds and levels of experience.
- Give and receive feedback with care.
- Focus criticism on ideas, code, and evidence.
- Assume good intent while addressing harm clearly.

## Unacceptable Behavior

- Harassment, threats, or personal attacks
- Doxxing or sharing private personal information
- Discriminatory language or behavior
- Encouraging misuse of verification reports
- Publishing real sensitive evidence in public issues without consent

## Reporting

For conduct issues, open a private security/contact channel once available. Until then, avoid posting private details publicly and contact project maintainers through GitHub.
