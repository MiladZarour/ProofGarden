# Safety and Ethics

ProofGarden does not prove absolute truth. It helps organize evidence, reveal signals, and document uncertainty.

## Careful Language

Reports should use cautious terms such as:

- likely
- unverified
- needs more evidence
- consistent with
- not enough evidence to conclude

Avoid overstating conclusions from weak signals. For example, missing EXIF metadata is common and should not be treated as proof that an image was manipulated.

## Misuse Boundaries

ProofGarden should not be used for:

- Harassment
- Doxxing
- Defamation
- Public shaming
- Identifying private people without consent
- Publishing private personal data

## Privacy

Users should avoid uploading private personal data unless they have a legitimate reason and appropriate consent. Sensitive uploads should not be exposed publicly by default. Future integrations that send evidence to external services must be explicit, documented, and opt-in.

## Human Review

Risk scores are transparent aids, not verdicts. Investigators should review the evidence, context, source reliability, and alternative explanations before sharing a report.
